What It Costs to Rent a Dump Trailer in Utah County

Real 2026 numbers — what the big box store up the road charges, what we charge, and what the whole job actually runs once the landfill gets paid.

HomeBlogWhat It Costs to Rent a Dump Trailer in Utah County

By Eagle Mountain Trailer Rental LLC · Updated August 2026

People ask what a dump trailer runs, and the honest answer is that it depends a lot on where you get it. More than you'd expect. So here are real numbers, including the big box store about ten minutes up the road from me.

What Home Depot charges

The Home Depot rental center in Saratoga Springs, store #4461, lists a 7'x14' dump trailer at these rates. I pulled these straight off their site on August 24, 2026. Their page notes that pricing is estimated and subject to change, and it varies by store, so check your own before you plan around it:

  • 4 hours: $187
  • Per day: $249
  • Per week: $747
  • 4 weeks: $1,868

What I charge

Same size trailer, 7 feet by 14 feet:

  • Per day: $80
  • Per week: $480
  • Per month: $1,200

The four-hour number is the one that gets me

Home Depot wants $187 to have that trailer for four hours. My full day is $80.

You could rent mine for two entire days, $160, and still come in $27 under what they charge for a single afternoon.

Per day they're running about three times my rate. That's $169 more, every day you have it.

Same size, different trailer

Footprint isn't the whole story. Their listing says 5,800 lbs maximum load capacity, though the spec table further down that same page says 3,500. I'll use the bigger number, since I'd rather be fair than win an argument.

Mine carries 9,800 lbs. It's a 14,000 lb GVWR trailer that weighs about 4,200 lbs empty.

So on an identical 7x14 footprint you're getting at least 4,000 more pounds of legal capacity. That matters most with material that's heavy for its size — dirt, gravel, concrete, wet sod. You can load mine full and still be under the rating. Load theirs full of the same stuff and you might not be. If you want the full breakdown, here's what a 7x14 dump trailer actually carries.

What Home Depot does better

I'm not going to pretend they don't have real advantages.

They deliver. I don't. If you need a trailer brought to your driveway or job site, they'll do it with a one-day minimum rental, and I genuinely can't help you there.

There are a lot of them. If you're not close to Eagle Mountain, a Home Depot probably is.

You can walk in. If one's sitting on the lot, you don't need a reservation.

Two things about their process worth knowing before you drive over: it's credit card only, no cash, and they run CLEAR identity verification against your driver's license before you can rent.

What the job actually costs

The rental is only part of the bill. Landfill fees run somewhere around $20 to $40 a load depending on what you're hauling.

A one-day garage cleanout with my trailer: $80 plus a landfill run. Call it $100 to $120 all in.

The same cleanout with theirs: $249 plus that same landfill run. So $269 to $289.

Same work, same dump fee, same size trailer, about $170 difference. If you're weighing this against a roll-off container instead, we compared those too: dump trailer vs. dumpster rental.

The honest bottom line

If you need it delivered, or you're nowhere near Eagle Mountain, go rent theirs. Those are real reasons and I'd rather say so than waste your afternoon.

If you've got a truck and you can make the drive, you're paying roughly a third as much for a trailer that hauls more. Booking is online, we're open 24/7, and there's nothing added on at pickup.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to rent a dump trailer in Utah County?
Our 7x14 dump trailer is $80/day, $480/week, or $1,200/month. For comparison, the Home Depot rental center in Saratoga Springs listed the same size trailer at $249/day when we checked on August 24, 2026. Competitor pricing varies by store and changes over time.
Is it cheaper to rent a dump trailer from Home Depot or a local company?
In Utah County, local is substantially cheaper. Our daily rate is $80 against the $249/day listed at the Saratoga Springs Home Depot as of August 2026 — roughly a third of the price. Home Depot does offer delivery, which we don't, so that can be worth the difference if you can't tow it yourself.
How much can a 7x14 dump trailer carry?
Ours carries 9,800 lbs on a 14,000 lb GVWR frame. The comparable Home Depot listing shows 5,800 lbs maximum load capacity in its highlights and 3,500 lbs in its spec table.
What do landfill fees cost on top of the rental?
Usually about $20 to $40 per load in Utah Valley, depending on the material. That's charged by the landfill, not by us.
What do I need to tow a dump trailer?
A 2-5/16" ball, a round 7-pin plug, and a working brake controller. Most half-ton pickups handle it for lighter loads, but a 3/4-ton is the safer call for dense material like dirt or concrete.
Do you deliver the dump trailer?
No. All of our rentals are pickup and return at our Eagle Mountain location. If you need delivery, Home Depot offers it with a one-day minimum rental.
