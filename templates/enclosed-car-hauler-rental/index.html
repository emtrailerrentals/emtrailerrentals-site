Can you haul a car inside the enclosed trailer?
Yes. The heavy-duty rear ramp door drops with an extension flap so a vehicle drives straight in, and the floor has D-ring tie-downs to strap it down. The 20-foot box with a V-nose front fits most cars, and everything stays out of the weather and out of sight.
What do I need to tow the 8.5×20 enclosed trailer?
A tow vehicle rated for the load. The trailer is rated at 9,900 lbs GVWR with a maximum load around 6,500 lbs, so a half-ton pickup with a tow package handles most jobs. It rides on dual drop-spring brake axles, which means your tow vehicle needs a working brake controller. See our tow rating guide if you're not sure.
Is this trailer good for moving house?
Yes. The 20-foot enclosed box, roughly 83–84" interior height and 36" side door make it practical for furniture, appliances and boxed goods, and the rear ramp saves lifting. Everything stays dry and locked up overnight, which an open trailer can't do. Our moving size guide walks through what fits.
Should I rent the enclosed car hauler or the open car hauler?
The open 8.5×22 car hauler is quicker to load, handles a heavier payload and suits fair-weather trips. This enclosed trailer protects the load from weather, road debris and prying eyes, which matters for a classic car, a long haul or a house move. If it needs to stay dry or out of sight, choose the enclosed one.
How is this different from the 24ft enclosed trailer?
This one is shorter at 20 feet and set up for driving a vehicle in, with a rear ramp door and a V-nose front. The 24ft enclosed trailer gives you more length and cargo room for bigger moves. Both are fully enclosed and lockable.
